How it works

                
For each palette in the corpus, the studio finds the colors that could play the parts of your locked ones, then turns its hues, scales its chroma and shifts its lightness so they land exactly on yours. The rest of that palette becomes the completion. Everything happens in OKLab.

                
A small neural network, trained to tell those palettes from random, rainbow and scrambled ones, ranks the results. Your  and  retrain its last layer, on this browser only.
