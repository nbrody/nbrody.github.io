The Hopf Fibration

Hopf (1931): the map S³ → S² sending a unit pair of complex numbers (z₁, z₂) to (2z₁z̄₂, |z₁|² − |z₂|²) is a fiber bundle whose fibers are great circles — one circle over each point of the 2-sphere — and any two distinct fibers form a Hopf link. This was the first example of a homotopically nontrivial map between spheres of different dimension (it generates π₃(S²) ≅ ℤ).
