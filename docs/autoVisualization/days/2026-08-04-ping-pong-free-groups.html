Ping-Pong and Free Groups

The ping-pong lemma: if A maps the complement of an interval Y into Y… more precisely, if all nonzero powers of A send Y into X and all nonzero powers of B send X into Y, for disjoint sets X, Y, then ⟨A, B⟩ is a free group. Sanov's classical instance: A = [[1, t],[0, 1]] and B = [[1, 0],[t, 1]] acting on ℝP¹ = ∂ℍ² by Möbius transformations, with Y = [−1, 1] and X its complement. The certificate works exactly when |t| ≥ 2 — drag the slider and watch the intervals collide. At t = 1 the pair generates all of SL(2,ℤ), which contains torsion, so freeness genuinely fails for small integers.
