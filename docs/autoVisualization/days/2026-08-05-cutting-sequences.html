Cutting Sequences on the Modular Surface

The Farey tessellation of the hyperbolic plane has an ideal triangle on each triple of rationals a/b, c/d, (a+c)/(b+d) with ad − bc = ±1; the modular group PSL(2,ℤ) permutes its triangles. Caroline Series' theorem: as the vertical geodesic from ∞ down to α crosses the tessellation, each triangle is exited to the left or right of the opposite vertex, and the resulting cutting sequence is La₁−1 Ra₂ La₃ Ra₄… where α = [0; a₁, a₂, a₃, …] is the continued fraction. Hyperbolic geometry computes Diophantine approximation. Press play: the view zooms with the geodesic, and the successive Farey triangles read out the digits.
