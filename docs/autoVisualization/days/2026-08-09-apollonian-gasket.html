The Apollonian Gasket

Descartes' circle theorem (1643): four mutually tangent circles with curvatures k1, k2, k3, k4 satisfy (k1+k2+k3+k4)² = 2(k1²+k2²+k3²+k4²). Fixing three circles, the two solutions for the fourth satisfy k4 + k′4 = 2(k1+k2+k3) — so swapping one circle for its mirror is an integral operation, and if the initial four curvatures are integers, every circle of the infinite packing has integer curvature. The swaps generate the Apollonian group, a thin subgroup of O(3,1), and which integers occur is governed by the (recently settled, negatively!) local–global conjecture.
