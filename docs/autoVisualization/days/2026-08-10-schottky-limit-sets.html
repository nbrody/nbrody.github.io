Schottky Groups and Their Limit Sets

Take two pairs of pairwise disjoint circles in the Riemann sphere. For a pair C = (c, r), C′ = (c′, r′) the Möbius map A(z) = c′ + λ/(z − c) with |λ| = rr′ carries C onto C′ and the exterior of C onto the interior of C′; the argument of λ is a free twist. Ping-pong on the four disks then shows ⟨A, B⟩ is discrete and free of rank two — a classical Schottky group — and that its limit set Λ is a Cantor set, the intersection of the nested level-k disks g1…gk−1(Dgk) drawn below. By Patterson–Sullivan dimH Λ equals the critical exponent δ(Γ), which Bowen's formula estimates as the root s of ∑|w|=k rws = 1.
