The Abelian Sandpile Identity

Put grains of sand on a grid; any cell holding 4 or more topples, sending one grain to each neighbor (grains fall off the edge). Dhar's theorem: the final stable configuration is independent of the order of toppling — the model is abelian — and the recurrent configurations form a finite abelian group under add-then-stabilize. The identity element of that group is not the empty configuration but an intricate fractal, computed here as (2σ − (2σ)°)° where σ is the all-3s configuration and ° denotes stabilization; Pegden–Smart proved its scaling limit exists.
