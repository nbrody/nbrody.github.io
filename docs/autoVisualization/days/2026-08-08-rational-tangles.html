Conway's Rational Tangles

A rational tangle is what you get from two horizontal strands by repeatedly twisting the two right-hand endpoints (T) and rotating the whole tangle a quarter turn (R). Conway's theorem (1970): assigning T: t ↦ t + 1 and R: t ↦ −1/t starting from 0 gives a fraction in ℚ ∪ {∞}, and two rational tangles are isotopic if and only if their fractions agree — the full topology is one rational number.
