Alexander Polynomials from Braid Closures

The reduced Burau representation β̄ : Bn → GLn−1(ℤ[t,t−1]) turns a braid into an integral matrix, and the Burau–Alexander theorem reads the Alexander polynomial of the closure β̂ straight off it: Δβ̂(t) ≐ det(I − β̄(β)) · (1 − t) / (1 − tn), an equality up to units ±tk. That the quotient is a genuine Laurent polynomial is part of the theorem, and it is checked here by exact division over ℤ. Markov's theorem says two braids close to the same link exactly when they differ by conjugations and stabilisations β ↦ βσn±1 — so those moves must leave Δ alone, which you can watch them do.
