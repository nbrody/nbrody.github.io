02 / THE PROCESS

A hand of
your own.

Handwriting is full of tiny decisions. This experiment tries to learn them from the way a pen moves.

Want to make one in your own style?

01

Gather your writing

Collect digital handwritten notes that preserve the pen strokes, along with the text they say.

02

Prepare the examples

Pair clean, legible lines with their transcriptions. More varied and accurate examples give the model more to learn from.

03

Train & experiment

Train or fine-tune a text-conditioned stroke model on a GPU, for example in Google Colab. Sample and review the results, then export it for inference to use it on a page like this one.

A work in progress, made one stroke at a time.
