Glass House Ball Machine

Thirty resin balls ride a chain lift to the crown of the machine. A tree of flip-flop switches sends each one down one of six routes: a marimba that plays “Ode to Joy”, a tower of bells, a loop-the-loop and ski jump, a water slide into a whirlpool, a glockenspiel plinko case, and a gong that waits for three balls at once.

The balls roll on real physics — rolling friction, banked rails, spin and skid, collisions — so no two minutes sound quite the same.
