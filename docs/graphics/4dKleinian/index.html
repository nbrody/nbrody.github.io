Coxeter data

Each entry is the relation between two mirrors: an integer m means they meet at angle π/m, ∞ means tangent, and d means disjoint at hyperbolic distance d. Any such choice with signature (4,1) is a discrete group.

While anything moves the frame is drawn at motion resolution; it refines as soon as everything is still.
