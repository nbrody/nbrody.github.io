Poincaré VR

Fundamental domains of Kleinian groups, certified by the same engine as the desktop Poincaré tool. Hold the Poincaré ball, pass through its faces, and step inside the tiling of hyperbolic space by copies of the domain.

  • 44 groups in the library
  • Dirichlet and Delaunay domains
  • Certificates and invariants
  • Walk inside the honeycomb

On the headset

  1. Open the Quest Browser and go to nbrody.github.io/vr.
  2. Choose an app, then press Enter VR, or Passthrough to stay in your room.
  3. With hands: pinch to press a button, pinch and hold to grab, use both hands to scale, or tap a panel with your index finger. With controllers: trigger to press, grip to grab.
Every app also runs in a desktop browser with a mouse. More tools: Geometry of Linear Groups · Topology
