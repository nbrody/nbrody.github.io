Cocompact Constructions

Two related explorations of geodesics and groups acting on the hyperbolic plane.

PSL₂(ℤ) Explorer

Explore modular tilings, generator axes and finite orbit samples in the upper half-plane.

PGL₂(ℤ[1/p]) Construction

Explore explicit conjugate geodesics, side-pairing strips and a virtual-bounding inequality.

These views explore different groups and constructions. The first uses finite numerical samples and does not certify the group’s convex core or quotient type.
