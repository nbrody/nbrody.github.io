Roots

A Pisot number is a real algebraic integer θ > 1 all of whose Galois conjugates lie strictly inside the unit circle. A Salem number is a real algebraic integer τ > 1 whose conjugates all lie on or inside the unit circle with at least one on it (so τ⁻¹ is a conjugate and the rest have modulus exactly 1). The plane is shaded by how fast Newton's method z ↦ z − p(z)/p′(z) converges; the basins of the conjugates tile ℂ in a fractal pattern. Click any point to watch its orbit find a conjugate.
