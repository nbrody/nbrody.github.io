Algebraic Tools

Explore the algebraic structure of linear groups through computational tools and visualizations. These tools focus on representation theory, character varieties, and algebraic group actions.

Number Rings

The ring generated by elements of a number field: orders, S-integers & indices

Minkowski Embeddings

Visualize Minkowski embeddings for ℤ[√2] and ℤ[1/3]

Zariski Closure

Zariski closures of subgroups of PGL₂(ℚ̄): trace field, quaternion algebra & S-arithmetic envelope

Quaternion Algebras

Ramification, maximal orders and unit groups of quaternion algebras over number fields

Expression Parser

Evaluate formulas exactly: closed forms, minimal polynomials, transcendence, and polynomial algebra

Trees of Hyperbolic Spaces

The Bruhat–Tits tree of ℚ((1/t)) with a hyperbolic plane at every vertex (balls over ℚ(i) and ℚ(ω)), and PGL₂ of the function field acting on it

Character Varieties

Compute and visualize character varieties for fundamental groups
