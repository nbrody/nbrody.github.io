Mathematical Context

The group \( B(\mathbb{Z}[1/n]) \) consists of upper-triangular matrices: \[ \begin{pmatrix} n^k & x \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \] where \( k \in \mathbb{Z} \) and \( x \in \mathbb{Z}[1/n] \).

Its action on the upper half-plane \( \mathbb{H}^2 \) is by affine conformal maps \( z \mapsto n^k z + x \). This preserves horizontal lines (horocycles at infinity).

The finitely generated subgroup generated by scaling \( b: z \mapsto nz \) and translation \( a: z \mapsto z+1 \) is isomorphic to the Baumslag-Solitar group: \[ BS(1, n) = \langle a, b \mid b a b^{-1} = a^n \rangle \]

In the Uniform 3D view, the sheets branch out along the Z-axis in an \( n \)-ary tree. In the Hyperbolic 2D view, the horizontal edges stretch exponentially showing the true hyperbolic scaling!
