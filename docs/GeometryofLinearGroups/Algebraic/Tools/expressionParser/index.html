Expression Parser

Type a formula. Algebraic numbers are computed exactly, with a closed form, minimal polynomial and field. Other values get a numeric approximation and, where a classical theorem applies, a proof of transcendence. Letters without values are indeterminates, so polynomials are expanded and factored.

Variables

A variable with a value is substituted; one without a value stays an indeterminate.

Input syntax and what is computed

Type naturally: / makes a fraction, ^ a power, sqrt and nthroot radicals, pi gives \pi, and zeta with a subscript gives \zeta_n = e^{2\pi i/n} (omega is \zeta_3). Juxtaposition multiplies. Functions: \sin, \cos, \tan, \sec, \csc, \cot, their inverses, \sinh, \cosh, \tanh, \exp, \ln, \log_b, \arg, \operatorname{Re}, \operatorname{Im}, |z|, \overline{z}, n!, \binom{n}{k}, \gcd and \operatorname{lcm}. The letters i and e are reserved. Pasted LaTeX and plain text such as sqrt(2)/2 also work.

Branches are principal, except that \sqrt[n]{a} with n odd and a < 0 is the real root; a^{1/n} is always principal.

Exact arithmetic happens in a number field K generated by the radicals and roots of unity that occur. Trigonometric values at rational multiples of \pi become sums of roots of unity, and |z|, \operatorname{Re}, \operatorname{Im} and \overline{z} are computed by adjoining complex conjugates. \pi, e and free letters are treated as indeterminates, which is legitimate because \pi and e are each transcendental. Logarithms and inverse trigonometric values are recognized when they are rational (multiples of \pi), and the recognition is verified exactly. Otherwise the theorems of Lindemann–Weierstrass, Hermite–Lindemann and Gelfond–Schneider certify transcendence when they apply.
