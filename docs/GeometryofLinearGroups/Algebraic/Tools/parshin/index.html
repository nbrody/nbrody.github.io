Matrix Generators

Elements of SL₂(ℤ[t]): 2×2 matrices whose entries are polynomials in t, of determinant 1. Click a generator under the picture to watch it act on the tree of planes; ⌘/Ctrl-click for its inverse.

Each plane shows its own Farey tessellation carried along by the current element, so the picture is that element applied to the tree.

The Tree of Planes

SL₂(ℤ[t]) acting on the Bruhat–Tits tree of ℚ((1/t)), with a hyperbolic plane at every vertex.

The picture

Put $\pi = 1/t$, so $\mathbb{Z}[t] \subset K = \mathbb{Q}((\pi))$, whose valuation ring $\mathbb{Q}[[\pi]]$ has residue field $\mathbb{Q}$. The link of each vertex of the tree of $\mathrm{SL}_2(K)$ is $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{Q})$, the rational cusps of a hyperbolic plane, so every vertex $v$ gets a plane $X(v)$ and every cusp of $X(v)$ carries the plane of a neighbour.

The base vertex $v_0$ is the lattice $\mathbb{Q}[[\pi]]^2$; its stabilizer is $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$, acting on $X(v_0)$ as usual. The plane at $p/q$ hangs off the cusp $p/q$ as its reflected Ford circle, joined by an edge of length $1/q$.

Frames and the action

A vertex is named by its path of cusps $w_1, \dots, w_n$ from $v_0$, and its plane is drawn in the frame $g_v = T_{w_1} \cdots T_{w_n}$ with $T_w = \begin{pmatrix} \pi & w \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, $T_\infty = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ \pi & 0 \end{pmatrix}$: the cusp $\infty$ of every plane points back to its parent.

An element $\gamma$ sends $v$ to $\gamma v$, and $g_{\gamma v}^{-1}\gamma\, g_v$ is, up to a power of $\pi$, in $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Q}[[\pi]])$; its reduction mod $\pi$ is the Möbius map $X(v) \to X(\gamma v)$. The animation moves every plane to its image while turning its contents by that map. All of it is computed exactly.

$\gamma$ translates an axis of the tree by $2\max(0, \deg \operatorname{tr}\gamma)$; otherwise it fixes a vertex and turns the tree about it.

Using it

Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom; click a plane to fly to it, and hover one to see its path from $v_0$. H hides the panel.
