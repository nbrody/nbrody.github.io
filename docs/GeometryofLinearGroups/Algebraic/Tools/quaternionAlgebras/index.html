Quaternion Algebras

Choose a number field K and a, b \in K^\times. The page finds where B = \left(\frac{a,\,b}{K}\right) ramifies, builds a maximal order \mathcal{O}, and describes its norm-one units \mathcal{O}^1. They form a finite group when B is totally definite, and otherwise an arithmetic lattice acting on a product of copies of \mathbb{H}^2 and \mathbb{H}^3.

Examples

Algebra

Enter f in x (use x itself for K = \mathbb{Q}), and a, b as rational expressions in the root, e.g. -1, t, (1 + t)/2.

What is computed, and how

Ramification. B ramifies at a real place where a and b are both negative. At an odd prime the tame symbol decides it, and at a dyadic prime a finite search for a zero of ax^2 + by^2 - z^2 modulo \mathfrak{p}^{2e+3}. The number of ramified places is even. The discriminant \mathfrak{D} is the product of the ramified primes.

Maximal order. The standard order \mathcal{O}_K\langle i, j\rangle is enlarged at each prime above 2ab. At an odd split prime a square root of a unit supplies the missing idempotent, and at dyadic primes the enlargement is found by search. The local orders are then glued into a \mathbb{Z}-basis. Maximality is certified by \operatorname{disc}_{\mathbb{Z}}\mathcal{O} = d_K^{4}\,N(\mathfrak{D})^2.

Units. If B is totally definite, \operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb{Q}}\operatorname{nrd} is positive definite, and by AM–GM its value [K:\mathbb{Q}] is attained exactly on \mathcal{O}^1, which is enumerated and identified. Otherwise P\mathcal{O}^1 is a lattice in \mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{R})^r \times \mathrm{PSL}_2(\mathbb{C})^s, cocompact unless B \cong M_2(K). Its covolume is given by Borel's formula \operatorname{vol} = \frac{2(4\pi)^r (2\pi^2)^s\,|d_K|^{3/2}\,\zeta_K(2)\prod_{\mathfrak{p} \mid \mathfrak{D}}(N\mathfrak{p}-1)}{(4\pi^2)^{[K:\mathbb{Q}]}}, where \zeta_K(2) comes from its Euler product. For K = \mathbb{Q} the signature of the Shimura curve comes from the counts of elliptic points. Sample units are short vectors for the majorant \sum_\sigma |\sigma x_0|^2 + |\sigma a||\sigma x_1|^2 + |\sigma b||\sigma x_2|^2 + |\sigma ab||\sigma x_3|^2.
