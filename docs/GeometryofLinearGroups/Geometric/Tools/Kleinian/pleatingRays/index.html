Plane

The √ρ plane shows the z for which ⟨(1 z; 0 1), (1 0; z 1)⟩ is discrete and free: conjugating by diag(√z, 1/√z) turns this pair into the Riley generators with ρ = z², so the picture is the pullback of the Riley slice under z ↦ z² — a double cover, symmetric under z ↦ −z, with cusps at ±2 and ±2i.

The dark region is (approximately) where ⟨X,Y⟩, X = [[1,1],[0,1]], Y = [[1,0],[ρ,1]], fails to be free and discrete; its complement is the Riley slice. Colored curves are the Keen–Series pleating rays: for each slope p/q, the branch of Φp/q−1((−∞,−2]) asymptotic to arg ρ = πp/q, traced inward from ∞ by Newton continuation — pairwise disjoint, landing on the p/q cusp. Dots are roots of the Farey polynomial Φp/q + 2. Extensions continue each ray past its cusp into the interior along Φp/q ∈ (−2, 2], where the p/q word is elliptic; the beads mark Φp/q = −2cos(π/n) — parameters of the Heckoid groups of slope p/q and order n, accumulating at the cusp as n → ∞. Dot placement uses double-precision Aberth iteration, so keep dot depth ≲ 28: the root cluster at ±4 is badly conditioned beyond that.
