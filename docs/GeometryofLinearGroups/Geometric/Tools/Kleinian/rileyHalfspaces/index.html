The Mathematics
Identify ℍ³ with quaternions q = ζ + t·j,   t > 0. A matrix g = [a b ; c d] ∈ SL(2,ℂ) acts by q ↦ (a q + b)(c q + d)⁻¹. The generator X = [1 z ; 0 1] fixes ∞ and translates by z; its invariant horoball is the half-space { t ≥ 1/|z| }. The generator Y = [1 0 ; z 1] fixes 0; its isometric sphere is the hemisphere centered at −1/z of radius 1/|z|, and Y⁻¹ gives the hemisphere at +1/z. Both are tangent to ∂ℍ³ at the fixed point 0.
Drag in the scene to orbit · scroll to zoom. The buttons animate the one-parameter parabolic flow gs, s: 0→1 of each generator on the basepoint, leaving a geodesic-horocyclic trail.
