GPS Calculator

Choose an arithmetic lattice and an immersed totally geodesic hypersurface . The calculator finds a congruence cover in which embeds, cuts the cover along it, and keeps the pieces. Pieces from incommensurable lattices glue into non-arithmetic manifolds (Gromov–Piatetski-Shapiro). In dimension 2, the hybrid picture draws two such tilings glued along a geodesic, and hybrids of triangle groups.
