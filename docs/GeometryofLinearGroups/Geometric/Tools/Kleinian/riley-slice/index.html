Plane

The √ρ plane shows the z for which ⟨(1 z; 0 1), (1 0; z 1)⟩ is discrete and free: conjugating by diag(√z, 1/√z) turns this pair into the Riley generators with ρ = z², so the picture is the pullback of the Riley slice under z ↦ z² — a double cover, symmetric under z ↦ −z, with cusps at ±2 and ±2i.

Riley's picture. Colour = number of faces of the Ford domain of ⟨X, E⟩, E(z) = 1/(ρz) — the Ford domain of ⟨X, Y⟩ taken at both cusps (E swaps ∞ and 0, E X E = Y). Computed exactly (power cells by half-plane clipping, faces closed under products) in parallel workers. First walls: |ρ| = 4 (white) and the cardioid |ρ| = 2(1 + |cos arg ρ|) (black); dashed, the one-cusp wall |ρ ∓ 2| = 2. In the √ρ plane these become the circle |z| = 2, the four circles |z ∓ 1| = 1, |z ∓ i| = 1, and two lemniscates.

The Ford domain of ⟨X, Y⟩ at ∞ alone, computed algebraically. Faces: powers Wk of the p/q Farey word, visible iff |Sk−1(Φp/q)| < |Sj−1| + |Sk−j−1| for all j (Chebyshev subadditivity; the k = 2 wall is |Φp/q(ρ)| = 2). Edges: which visible isometric circles overlap, |C−C′−n| < r+r′ — crossing an edge wall relinks the face graph without changing the faces, carving each fan into the curvilinear panels of Jorgensen's picture. Colour = face pairs, shade = linking class. Its first wall is the Lyndon–Ullman circle |ρ − 2| = 2, not Riley's |ρ| = 4: it misses the faces of the cusp at 0.

The dark region is (approximately) where ⟨X,Y⟩, X = [[1,1],[0,1]], Y = [[1,0],[ρ,1]], fails to be free and discrete; its complement is the Riley slice. Colored curves are the Keen–Series pleating rays: for each slope p/q, the branch of Φp/q−1((−∞,−2]) asymptotic to arg ρ = πp/q, traced inward from ∞ by Newton continuation — pairwise disjoint, landing on the p/q cusp. Dots are roots of the Farey polynomial Φp/q + 2. Extensions continue each ray past its cusp into the interior along Φp/q ∈ (−2, 2], where the p/q word is elliptic; the beads mark Φp/q = −2cos(π/n) — parameters of the Heckoid groups of slope p/q and order n, accumulating at the cusp as n → ∞. The Ford regions reproduce Riley's original picture: the colour counts the faces of the Ford domain of ⟨X, E⟩, E(z) = 1/(ρz), which swaps the two cusps ∞ and 0 and conjugates X to Y — that is, the Ford domain of ⟨X, Y⟩ taken at both of its cusps (Riley's palette: 1 black, 3 blue, 5 green, 7 red, …). Its first walls are |ρ| = 4, where the isometric circle of E meets its translate, and the cardioid |ρ| = 2(1 + |cos arg ρ|), where the circles of E, EX⁻¹ and Y⁻¹ are concurrent; each later wall is a vertex of the Ford domain reaching the sphere at infinity, and the walls pile up at every cusp. “Cusp ∞ only” shows the Ford domain of ⟨X, Y⟩ at ∞ alone, whose walls are algebraic: the faces over the p/q arc are powers of the Farey word, Wk visible iff |Sk−1(Φp/q)| < |Sj−1| + |Sk−j−1| for 0 < j < k (Chebyshev Sm), so each fan opens on |Φp/q(ρ)| = 2; its first wall is the Lyndon–Ullman circle |ρ − 2| = 2. Dot placement uses double-precision Aberth iteration, so keep dot depth ≲ 28: the root cluster at ±4 is badly conditioned beyond that.
