Face Pairings

Each codimension-1 face of the domain is paired to another face by a group element, written as a word in your generators. Faces of the stabilizer cone are paired by rotations fixing the basepoint.

Click a generator to animate its isometry (⌘/Ctrl-click for the inverse). Double-click a face of the polyhedron to apply its pairing.

How it Works

The tool computes a canonical Dirichlet domain for the group generated by your matrices, verifies Poincaré's polyhedron conditions numerically (face pairings + edge cycles), and reports the standard geometric generators.
