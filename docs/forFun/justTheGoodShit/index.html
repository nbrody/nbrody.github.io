FAQ

Wait — so I never poop again?

Correct. The JTGS™ Core intercepts the meal before your body ever has to. Whatever would have eventually become waste is diverted at the source and sent to your home's existing plumbing. You simply absorb the good shit.

Where does the bad sh*t go?

Down the drain, via the included MacroSieve™ outflow hose. Think of it as outsourcing the least fun 40 seconds of your day to a quiet appliance on your counter.

Does it hurt?

Our legal team has asked us to describe the sensation as "novel." Most users report a gentle, warm reabsorption. A small minority report "profound philosophical unease," which fades by week two.

Can I still taste my food?

Absolutely. FlavorLoop™ re-injects the full taste profile during reabsorption. You get all the joy of the meal and none of the aftermath. Some say it tastes better the second time, which we are choosing not to think about.

Is this real?

No. This is a joke. Please continue using your digestive system as intended. It's doing great.
