What you're seeing

The Klein four-group V = (ℤ/2)² has three nonzero elements a, b, c, and any two of them add up to the third: a + b = c, x + x = 0. Every live cell here is one of them.

Whether a cell lives is plain Life, decided by how many live neighbours it has, whatever their colours. So the field's black-and-white shadow is ordinary Life. Colour decides only which element a newborn is. Under Heredity it is the sum of its three parents, so two a and a b make b, and parents a, b, c sum to 0: the birth is stillborn.

Alchemy lets survivors absorb their neighbours' sum. Predation uses the order-3 automorphism σ : a → b → c → a of V to give every colour a predator. Tribes lets the majority colour win. The lens recolours the display by a quotient V/⟨g⟩ ≅ ℤ/2 without touching the state.
