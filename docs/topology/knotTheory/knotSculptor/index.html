Knot

Seifert surface

The surface is a level set of the solid angle $\Omega$ the knot subtends. Its two sides get two colors: it is orientable. Sweep runs through every level.

Invariants

Projection

The globe colors each viewing direction by how many crossings that projection has; the simplest views are teal. Click it to look from there.

On a Quest

  • Draw: pinch with your right hand and move. Let go and the loop closes.
  • Turn it: pinch with your left hand, anywhere, and turn your wrist. Both hands: scale and spin.
  • Menu: turn your left palm toward your face and poke the buttons with your right index finger.
  • Globe: poke a color on the globe to see the knot from that direction.
