Draw: with nothing on screen, pinch with your right hand and move. Sparks fly and the rope cycles through the palette; let go and the loop closes and smooths its color period.

Turn it: pinch and drag with either hand; two hands scale it.

Menu: turn your left palm toward your face and poke the buttons with your right index finger.

Viewing sphere: each chamber shows how many crossings the knot has from that direction (teal = fewest). Click or poke to view from there.

Projection

The knot diagram you see from where you stand. The viewing sphere colors each direction by its crossing count; teal is simplest.

Seifert surface

A level set of the solid angle $\Omega$ the knot subtends. Its two sides get two colors: it is orientable.
