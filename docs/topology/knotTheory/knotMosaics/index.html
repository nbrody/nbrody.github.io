Knot Mosaics

A knot mosaic (Lomonaco–Kauffman) is a square grid of tiles, each carrying arcs, a straight strand or a crossing. When every strand end meets a strand end in the neighbouring tile, the tiles join up into a knot or link diagram.

Surfaces

On the disk no strand may leave the grid. The other surfaces glue edges of the square, drawn dashed with arrows in matching colours. The cylinder glues left to right straight across, and the torus also glues top to bottom; the Möbius band glues left to right with a flip, and the projective plane flips both pairs. The sphere is an unfolded cube: edges of the same colour meet when it is folded up.

Placing tiles

  • Pick a tile in the palette (or press 0–9, V for the virtual crossing), then click or drag across cells. Drag a palette tile onto a cell to drop it there.
  • Right-click a cell to erase it. ⌘/Ctrl-click toggles a cell in the selection; ← → rotate the selected tiles, Delete empties them, Esc clears the selection.
  • Draw mode (D): drag across cells and the right tiles appear along the path. Crossing an existing strand makes a crossing with the new strand on top; closing the loop ends the stroke.
  • Grid Size keeps your drawing, centred. On a surface with glued edges, strands that cross a glued edge are carried straight through the new margin to stay joined; shrinking crops the edges evenly, and ⌘Z brings them back.
  • ⌘Z undo, ⇧⌘Z redo, C clear, F fit the grid to the window.

View in ℝ³

A disk mosaic opens straight in the 3D smoother. A sphere or torus mosaic is first folded up in 3D (Unfold flattens it again, Tiles/Tubes switch between the tile faces and strand tubes); Smooth then hands the folded strands to the smoother. There, Smooth starts lowering the chosen knot energy (Möbius, Coulomb, ropelength, …) and Pause stops it. The other surfaces have no 3D view yet. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
