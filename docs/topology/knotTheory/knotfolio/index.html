Using KnotFolio

Drawing

Drag on the canvas with the pencil. A stroke that runs into an existing line goes over it and leaves a gap in the line underneath. Hold Shift while drawing to go under instead, or pick a pencil mode: over, through (no automatic gaps) or under.

Drag with the right mouse button, or choose the eraser, to erase. Gaps are only bridged between strands of the same colour, so colours keep the components of a link apart.

Clean up thins every stroke to its core line; Thin and Thicken remove or add one boundary pixel.

Tracing an image

Use Load image, drop an image onto the window, or paste one. In image mode, drag to crop (or choose the move tool, or drag with the right button, to move it), scroll to rescale, and adjust the blur, adaptive radius and threshold until the strands come out cleanly. Accept takes the cropped selection into painting mode.

Diagrams

Convert to diagram analyses the picture. If some junction cannot be read, usually because an understrand has fused to the overstrand, it is marked in red; a little erasing fixes it.

In diagram mode, choose a tool and click the diagram: change a crossing, toggle a virtual crossing, reverse a component, delete a component, or recolour it. Beautify redraws the diagram with a Tutte embedding; drag empty space to move the diagram and scroll to zoom. The panel lists crossings, components, writhe, bridges, genera and the PD and DT codes. Convert to drawing goes back to free-form editing.

Undo and redo cover every step, including mode changes.
