How it Works

A basis of TLn is the set of planar matchings of n top and n bottom points (Catalan many). A product x·y stacks x on top of y; every closed loop that forms is erased and contributes a factor δ.

Coefficients are exact rational functions. In ℚ(A) the loop value is δ = −A² − A⁻², and the braid generator σi = A + A⁻¹ei gives the Kauffman bracket, hence the Jones polynomial, of a braid closure.

The pills at the bottom multiply the element by a generator: click for x·g, ⌘/Ctrl-click for g·x, ⇧-click a σ for σ⁻¹.

Invariants

Traces, braid closures, structure, the action on cell modules and the minimal polynomial of the current element.
