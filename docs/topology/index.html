Topology Tools

Interactive tools for knots, diagrammatic algebra, graphs and low-dimensional topology.

Knots

KnotFolio

Draw knot diagrams by painting strands, or trace them from an image

Knot Mosaic Playground

Place tiles on a disk, sphere or torus to build knot diagrams, then view them in ℝ³

Knot Sculptor

Draw a knot in the air with your hands on a Meta Quest (or with a mouse), then see its invariants, its projections and a Seifert surface

Knot Energy Minimizer

Embed a knot diagram in ℝ³ and watch it relax by gradient descent on discrete Möbius energy

Spatial Graph Relaxer

Draw, type or load a graph in ℝ³ and let it settle, never passing through itself, into a symmetric embedding

Diagrammatic algebra

Temperley–Lieb Calculator

Build elements of TLn(δ) from generators, braids and Jones–Wenzl projectors

Webs & Foams

Edit pairs of planar webs, and build, reshape and inspect the foam cobordisms between them

Graphs and surfaces

Planar Graph Lab

Planar graphs, their flow polynomials, and the golden identity

Theta × Theta

Remove the diagonal squares from Θ × Θ and see the genus-2 surface that is left

Mapping class groups

Curves & Braids

Draw a curve in a punctured disk, pull it tight to its hyperbolic geodesic, and braid the punctures to carry it along

Curves & Twists

Draw curves on a closed surface of genus 2–5, in the hyperbolic polygon or on a pretzel in 3D, pull them tight to geodesics, watch Dehn twists act, and check a presentation of the mapping class group

Geometry

Hopf Fibration

The map from S³ to S², its fibres projected into ℝ³ by stereographic projection
