Ant Colony

No ant knows where the food is. Each one wanders, and when it stumbles onto a crumb it hauls it home — laying a scent trail as it goes. Other ants follow the strongest trail, so a good route reinforces itself while unused ones evaporate. The nest gives off its own scent that foragers home in on. Shortest paths emerge from the chemistry — paint food, walls, or erase directly on the world.

About

This is stigmergy: coordination through traces left in the environment rather than direct communication. The green trail is a pheromone ants lay only while carrying food, and it evaporates over time — because shorter round-trips get reinforced faster than they fade, the colony converges on efficient routes without any ant planning one. The faint blue haze is the nest's own scent: a gradient laden ants follow home, bending around any wall in the way. Raise evaporation to make trails skittish; lower it to lock them in.
