How it works

Thousands of mass tracers are born in a tiny, hot region and flung outward with a Hubble-like velocity, filling a periodic (wrap-around) cube. Gravity is solved on a 3D grid with a particle–mesh scheme: matter is deposited into cells (cloud-in-cell), the density field is smoothed to a softened potential, and each particle is accelerated down its gradient toward over-dense regions. An expansion-drag term damps peculiar velocities so structure freezes out — letting the initial ripples collapse into sheets, filaments, and clusters: the cosmic web. Rendered with three.js points, additive blending, and an Unreal-bloom glow. Color tracks blackbody-style temperature, local density, or speed.
